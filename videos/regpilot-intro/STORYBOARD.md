## Video direction

- concept: the letter under the lamp. One fictional BSA sentence is the only object. A sage wash travels the line. Failing words isolate in amber or coral; the rest stays ink on paper.
- palette: paper `#EEF2F6`, ink `#0E1A2B`, sage `#0F766E`, amber `#B45309`, coral `#B91C1C`. No purple, no black field, no vignette.
- type: Fraunces for the sentence, Manrope for the name, IBM Plex Mono for one ledger word (`STOPPED`). No topbar, no favicon, no tiles.
- motion: the camera is still. The lamp reads left along the first line, drops to the second, and stops on the hazard. Hard cuts. Reveals are paced inside each shot.
- sentence: "Six SARs were filed past sixty days, with no lookback and no escalation." Fictional sample, no bank name.
- negative: no giant slogan, no desk chrome, no identical dissolves, no push-in.
